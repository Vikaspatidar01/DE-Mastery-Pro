📱 Install DE Mastery Pro

DE Mastery Pro is a Progressive Web App (PWA). You can install it on your Laptop (Windows/Mac) and Mobile Phone (Android/iOS) to use it offline anytime like a native desktop/mobile app!

💻 On Laptop (Chrome or Microsoft Edge):

1. Look at your browser's address bar (top-right next to URL/bookmark star).
2. Click the 'Install app' icon (looks like a monitor with down arrow) or click the Install App button below.
3. Click Install. The app will open in its own clean window with a desktop shortcut!

📱 On Android (Chrome):

1. Open the app in Chrome.
2. Tap the 3 dots menu (⋮) in the top right.
3. Tap 'Install app' or 'Add to Home screen'.

🍏 On iPhone / iPad (Safari):

1. Open this page in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap 'Add to Home Screen'.
